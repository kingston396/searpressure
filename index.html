Sear Pressure

A cooking game by Ragnarok Talent Partners LLC

Navy shipmates Hunter and Riley take their friend Reyes's food truck coast to coast to live out his dream. Chop, cook, plate and serve across 35 kitchens from Route 66 to New Orleans to New York, play online co-op or versus with 2–4 players, and take on a new Daily Challenge every day. On Android, earn achievements on Google Play Games.
