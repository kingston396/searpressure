Sear Pressure Privacy Policy

Ragnarok Talent Partners LLC · Effective 24 September 2026

Sear Pressure is a cooking game published by Ragnarok Talent Partners LLC ("we", "us"). We built it so that it doesn't need anything about you to work. This page explains exactly what the game does and doesn't do with information.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no name or email asked for.
  • Your progress (stars, coins, outfits, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads (details below).
  • An optional one-time purchase, "Remove ads", turns the ads off. It's bought through Google Play, which handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you're asked for consent first. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet: stars and best scores, coins, items bought in the in-game shop (with in-game coins only), chef outfits, story progress, the daily challenge, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and left-handed controls. This never leaves your device. We can't see it, and deleting the app deletes it.

Information collected by the game engine

Sear Pressure is made with the Unity engine. When a Unity game starts, the engine may send Unity Technologies limited, anonymous technical information about the device, such as the device model, operating system version, graphics hardware, screen size and language. Unity uses it to improve the engine. It does not include your name, contacts, location or anything you do in the game. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad now and then between levels, and optional ads you can choose to watch to continue a failed level or to double the coins from a level. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them and measure how they perform, and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location worked out from your IP address;
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language, screen) and about the ads you see and tap;
  • crash and performance information about the ad software.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

The "Remove ads" purchase

If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play processes the payment under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The game only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, and remembers the answer on your device so ads stay off offline. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. After a purchase, the game stops loading ads, so AdMob's data collection described above stops too.

Permissions

The game uses the internet (to load ads and check your purchase), the advertising ID permission (for Google AdMob, above), Google Play's billing permission (for the optional purchase) and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's Settings. It does not ask for your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

Sear Pressure is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. We don't collect personal information ourselves at all; the advertising information described above goes to Google. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, or you have any question, contact us below.

Online play in future versions

A future update may add optional online play with friends. If it does, this policy will be updated before that version is released. It will explain what is sent (such as a temporary room code and the game moves between phones) and how long it is kept.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
