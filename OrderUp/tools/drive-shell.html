Delivery Runs: drive the orders out yourself. In the full game these are secret bonuses found by three-starring the delivery kitchens. Here all three are open.

Drag on the left to drive: up is gas, down is brake, sideways steers. Hold Drift (or Space) to slide. Follow the arrow and stop in the green ring to hand a bag over. After three bags, reload at the food truck. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space to drift, Esc to pause.
