Delivery Runs: drive the orders out yourself. In the full game these are secret bonuses found by three-starring the delivery kitchens. Here all three are open.

Drag on the left to drive: up is gas, down is brake, sideways steers. Hold Drift (or Space) to slide. New to it? Take the Driving lesson first (Run 1 starts with it the first time). Follow the arrow (and the corner map) and stop in the green ring to hand a bag over. After three bags, reload at the food truck. Knocking someone over costs 10 coins. Watch for road works, crashes, potholes and oil. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space to drift, Esc to pause.
