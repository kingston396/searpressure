How to play

  • StickDrag anywhere on the left half of the screen to walk. You act on the counter you're facing.
  • GrabPick up and put down. Take from crates, drop on counters, add food to plates and pots, serve at the bell.
  • ChopChop raw food on a board. Your chef keeps chopping until they're done or you walk away.
  • SwapSwitch chefs. Start one chopping, then swap and cook with the other.

Every order has a timer, and faster service earns bigger tips. If a ticket runs out, you lose coins. Pots and pans burn if you leave them too long. Put anything in the bin to empty it.

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to grab, E to chop, Q to swap, Esc to pause.
